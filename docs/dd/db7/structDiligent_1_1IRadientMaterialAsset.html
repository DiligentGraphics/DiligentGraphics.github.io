Detailed Description

Definition-backed material asset with writable initialization state.

Writers may be used to initialize the asset before its load status, GPU resource status, or render view is first queried. Runtime mutation after one of those queries is not currently supported. This restriction is not enforced by the API. Material assets and their writers are not thread-safe. The caller is responsible for synchronizing all access to them.
